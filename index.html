Raja Magadh privacy policy

20 November 2026 · Android · com.piyadasistudios.rajamagadh

Arif Raza develops Raja Magadh — Niyati: The Land Remembers under the personal studio name Piyadasi Studios. This policy describes the game from Build 214. Earlier testing versions offered fewer online features. For questions or privacy requests, email piyadasistudios@gmail.com.

Your offline campaign

All eleven episodes are free to play. You can play and load local progress without signing in. The game stores your campaign, episode-start position, lives, in-game currency, discoveries, unlocks, eligible scores and milestones, preferences and tutorial choices on your device. Where needed, it keeps local recovery copies. Your game progress and ownership of Premium are separate.

Optional Google Play Games features

If you connect, Google authenticates your Play Games profile. The game uses a player identifier to associate scores, achievements and backups with the correct profile. It remembers your connection choice and can reconnect on later launches. Google may offer to create a Play Games profile through its own interface. Your gamer name and avatar are managed by Google.

While connected, the game sends eligible cumulative scores and episode achievements to Google. It can keep eligible progress on your device while offline and submit it after you reconnect. A leaderboard or achievement screen can display scores, ranks, achievements and a gamer identity to other people according to Google’s features and your profile settings. Choosing to stop sharing in the game ends future game submissions until you connect again. Data Google has already received may remain in your Google account.

Manual backup and restore

From the main menu, you can ask Google Play Games to store a separate copy of your campaign. A backup includes campaign progress, discoveries, score history, a player identifier, an integrity checksum and a description with the episode and score. It excludes Premium ownership and reviewer access. Finding and previewing a backup reads saved-game data from Google. The game checks the account and the save contents. Applying a restore requires your confirmation and the game first preserves a local recovery copy. Conflicts or a restore that would discard recognized progress can prevent restoration. Upload and restore are player-initiated. A successful local write can precede Google’s remote synchronization.

Premium purchases

One optional, non-consumable purchase unlocks the Premium features currently available in Raja Magadh. If another volume is released as an update to this game, the same purchase covers its Premium features at no additional charge. Google Play presents localized prices, handles payment and keeps its purchase records. The Google Play Billing library connects on Android startup and checks product details and existing purchases. It can check again on resume, periodically and when you select Restore Purchases, even if you have never bought Premium.

On your device the game checks the package, product, purchase state and purchase token returned by Play. For a confirmed purchase it grants access and sends acknowledgement through Google Play. It uses the token temporarily and keeps a protected digest on your device to recognize ownership offline. It rechecks ownership when Play becomes available. A confirmed loss of ownership, including after a refund or when a different Play account is used, can remove access. Google Play can restore a purchase for the account that owns it after an update or reinstall.

The Studio operates no purchase-verification server and the game does not send your purchase token to a Studio endpoint. The game does not ask for your payment-card details, Google password or a separate Studio account. Google may make order, refund, sales and financial information available to the Studio through its developer and merchant tools. Please keep payment details and purchase tokens out of support email.

Google services and diagnostics

Google’s Billing and Play Games software can process technical information, such as the app and SDK versions, operation status and timing, device or service context and identifiers used to associate related operations. Some Billing diagnostics can be queued and sent without a purchase. Google describes using Play Games service data and diagnostics for functionality, reliability and product improvement. Manual backup and rankings send the game information described above. Google applies its own privacy practices to its services. Play may also process distribution, installation, security and reliability information.

Build 214 includes no advertising or Studio-operated gameplay analytics. There is no Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics service in this build. Basic encoding libraries used by Google’s SDKs package data for transport. They do not create a Studio Firebase service.

Storage and safeguards

Local records stay in the game’s private app storage until you or the operating system remove them, or game operations update or replace them. The purchase cache uses a per-installation key held by Android. The identified Google network services use encrypted connections. Access controls, checksums and encryption reduce specific risks. Google controls retention of data held by its services and may retain records for purposes such as fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

  • Local progress. Android’s app settings let you clear Raja Magadh’s storage. This can permanently erase the campaign, local recovery copies and settings on that device. An existing Google backup is separate. Uninstalling the game can also erase local saves.
  • Google Play Games. Use your Google account’s Play Games data controls to delete available data for this game or your Play Games profile. Google explains which records its controls cover. Stopping sharing in the game does not perform remote deletion. Manage or delete Play Games data.
  • Purchases. Use your Google Play purchase history and Google Play’s refund procedures for transaction records and eligible refund requests. Clearing local storage does not cancel ownership. Google Play refund information.
  • Studio support records. To request access to or deletion of a message or personal information you sent to the Studio, email piyadasistudios@gmail.com with “Raja Magadh privacy request” and enough information to identify the correspondence. We handle requests manually and may ask for reasonable verification. We keep correspondence as needed to handle support, disputes and legal obligations. The Studio cannot directly erase data under Google’s control or files on a device it does not possess.

Audience and updates

The game is intended for ages 13 and above. A parent or guardian can contact the Studio with a privacy concern. The date at the top identifies this policy revision. We update this page when the game’s data practices change.
