Raja Magadh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Raja Magadh — Niyati: The Land Remembers is developed and operated by Arif Raza under the personal studio name Piyadasi Studios, which is not a separately registered company. For privacy questions or requests, email piyadasistudios@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android game with package identifier com.piyadasistudios.rajamagadh.

Current testing status and upcoming features

The latest prepared Batch 20 source includes optional Google Play Games authentication, achievements, a leaderboard and manual Saved Games backup/restore. The achievement and leaderboard resources have been created as drafts. These features have not yet been verified against live Google services or delivered as the final consolidated Android build. Their description below explains the implemented workflow when made available; it does not mean that an older installed testing build already includes them.

The earlier version 208 testing build did not enable Play Games submission or new Premium purchases. In the latest prepared configuration, new purchases remain disabled and the Studio's purchase-verification service is not deployed or connected. Automatic Google Play Billing checks are separate and can still operate. We will revise this policy before activating services that materially change these data practices.

Offline play and information on your device

The campaign and local saves remain available independently of optional Play Games features. Connecting to Play Games is not required to play all eleven episodes. Online services need a connection; completing a purchase or revalidating a purchase cannot be guaranteed offline.

The game stores campaign progress, episode-start state, lives, currency, discoveries, unlocks, scores, preferences, tutorial status and local diagnostic/recovery records. The prepared online features also keep eligible milestones, pending submissions, account-specific acknowledgments, a Play Games player identifier and campaign/account associations. A hashed player identifier binds a campaign to its selected account; hashing does not make that association anonymous. Where applicable, Premium entitlement and reviewer-access status are stored locally.

Local files rely on operating-system protections; the game does not add its own encryption layer to save files. Clearing app storage can permanently erase local progress and recovery copies. Ordinary Android app backup is disabled in the configuration, but device-transfer behavior can vary. Do not assume that clearing data or changing installations will restore your journey.

Optional Google Play Games

In builds containing this feature, choosing Connect to Play Games starts the integration. The game remembers that choice and may reconnect on later launches or returns to the app. It obtains your game-specific Play Games player ID to associate submissions and backups with the correct player. It does not create a separate Piyadasi Studios player account.

Google manages your Play Games profile, including your gamer name and avatar. The game's bridge uses the player ID; Google's screens may display profile information. This integration does not request your Google email address, friends list, server authentication code or access to ordinary Google Drive files.

Achievements and overall scores

The game prepares achievements for legitimate campaign completion of Episodes 1, 3, 6, 9 and 11. With Play Games sharing enabled, it sends achievement identifiers and unlock requests, and the best committed cumulative eligible score, to Google for your authenticated player. Pending progress is retained locally and retried when connected. The score uses one Shatamana as ten Karshapana-equivalent points. Buying Premium alone does not disqualify legitimate progress; assisted and temporary modes are excluded from these submissions.

The single leaderboard is “Volume I — Overall Score.” Your score, ranking, gamer name, avatar and associated profile/game information may be visible to other players through Google's services, subject to Google's profile visibility controls. Campaign backup contents are not intentionally published to the leaderboard. Review your Play Games privacy settings.

Optional Saved Games backup and restore

Cloud Backups is a manual, confirmed backup-and-restore feature using Google Play Games Saved Games. It is not continuous automatic merging of your devices. A requested backup sends the committed campaign state, player ID, game/package and format information, an integrity checksum and descriptive backup metadata to Google. Campaign state includes episode-start resources and lives, discoveries, unlocks, completed scoring events, currency collection records and Chronicle progress. It does not include purchase tokens, Premium entitlement leases, reviewer codes, preferences, signing material or cover screenshots.

Google's SDK manages transfer and may cache or queue a backup while offline. An accepted backup operation is not proof that another device can already retrieve it. Only progress included in your last requested committed backup is covered.

Restore requires confirmation and account/integrity checks. With existing local progress, the implementation checks for a compatible continuation of the same campaign and rejects older or conflicting progress rather than silently merging it. It retains separate backups and does not automatically resolve unexpected conflicts by overwriting one side. Before an accepted restore, the game creates and verifies local recovery copies. These protections are designed to reduce data loss; they are not a guarantee against every failure.

Stopping sharing and deleting game data

Stop sharing turns off the game's Play Games requests; it does not sign you out of Google's device-wide account, delete existing Google records or necessarily cancel already queued SDK transfers or diagnostics. Local campaign and recovery files remain separate.

Google provides controls to delete an individual game's Play Games data or your Play Games profile. These controls do not delete the Studio's support correspondence or local game files. Retained local milestones and scores may be submitted again if you reconnect. The game does not currently provide automatic cloud-backup cleanup or an in-game cloud-delete function.

Google Play Billing and Premium

The public Android configuration connects to Google Play Billing at startup. It requests product details and existing purchase status on connection, on returning to the app, periodically and when Restore Purchases is used. These requests can occur without initiating a purchase.

Premium is intended as one optional, non-consumable, one-time purchase, not a subscription. Google operates the payment interface and associated account/payment processing. The game can receive product IDs, localized prices, purchase status, purchase tokens, order IDs and transaction details. It does not receive your payment-card number or present its own card-entry form.

Planned verification service — not currently active

The prepared verification design would send a purchase token over HTTPS to a Studio-controlled verification endpoint. That service would check purchase status with Google, acknowledge eligible purchases and return a signed entitlement to support Premium access, restoration, retries and later revalidation, including revoked or refunded purchases. The game would store a signed, time-limited entitlement and token hash locally for temporary offline use. This is not a promise of indefinite offline ownership verification.

No such endpoint is currently configured. No purchase token is sent to it, and new purchase flows remain blocked. Before activation, we will disclose the actual hosting/processing arrangements and relevant retention practices. The intended request contains the purchase token, not campaign saves or Play Games player IDs. A deployed host would also receive connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata. The prepared application code avoids logging raw tokens, but hosting and infrastructure logging still require configuration and review.

Google SDK information and third-party processing

Google's SDKs can process technical information in addition to player-requested operations. Billing operational reporting can include operation/error events, app and library versions, Android/device model and build, memory, language/country settings, network type/operator, timing and a client-instance correlation value. Such reporting can occur without a purchase when the reporting service is available. Network recipients can see connection information such as IP addresses. A device build fingerprint describes an operating-system build, not a biometric fingerprint.

Google discloses automatic analytics and diagnostics for Play Games SDK stability and product improvement. Feature-dependent gameplay and account information is processed when those services are used. This is distinct from a custom Studio analytics system. The presence of a bundled transport or encoding library alone does not establish that it independently sends information.

Google handles its services under its Privacy Policy and retention practices. See also Google's Play Games SDK disclosures. Those disclosures describe Google's latest SDK practices, not a packet-by-packet verification of this testing build.

Information received by Piyadasi Studios

The prepared build has no deployed Studio purchase-verification endpoint, separate player database or Studio-hosted cloud-save service. Optional Play Games data goes to Google rather than a custom Studio server. Google Play may provide developer reports through Play Console; this is separate from creating a custom analytics backend.

If you email us, we and the email providers handling the message receive your email address, message and any name or attachments you include. Contact Us opens your chosen email app; it does not automatically send a message or attach saves. We use correspondence to respond to requests and address related issues. Send only what is necessary, and never send passwords, payment-card details or private keys.

Retention and privacy requests

Local information remains until overwritten or removed by existing game functions or device/app-storage controls. Recovery copies have no automatic deletion schedule in this implementation. Removing local data does not remove Google-held backups, scores, transaction records or profile data, and deleting Google data does not necessarily erase local files.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, related issues and applicable legal obligations, without an invented fixed retention deadline or automatic-deletion promise. Google and email providers apply their own retention practices; we do not promise a specific retention period for their records.

You can request access, correction or deletion of information we hold by contacting piyadasistudios@gmail.com. We will review your request, verify identity only where necessary and explain any applicable retention constraints. We cannot delete every record held by Google on your behalf. Depending on your location and applicable law, additional rights may include objection, restriction and complaints to a data-protection authority.

Security and international processing

Google describes HTTPS protection for Play Games data in transit; the Billing reporting destination identified in the audit uses HTTPS. The planned verifier requires HTTPS and validates signed entitlement responses. These safeguards do not encrypt every local file, prove every third-party communication was independently tested, or guarantee absolute security. Checksums detect changes or corruption; they are not a replacement for access controls.

Google provides app distribution, Billing and optional Play Games services; email providers handle correspondence. Their processing may occur outside your country under their applicable practices. Visiting a hosted copy of this policy also sends ordinary connection information to its hosting provider. This HTML contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies or externally loaded fonts; hosting-platform behavior is separate.

Advertising and intended audience

Raja Magadh contains no advertisements or subscriptions. Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are not integrated, and no custom gameplay-analytics upload has been added. These statements do not exclude the Google SDK processing described above.

The intended audience is ages 13 and above. This does not mean the game verifies ages or that age 13 is adulthood in every jurisdiction. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns can contact us. The content rating is determined separately through the platform's classification process.
